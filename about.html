About the club

We're a student-run club that meets every week to speak English together in a relaxed, no-pressure setting. No tests, no grades. Just conversation, games, and learning from each other.

Who can join

Anyone who wants to improve. Beginners to advanced speakers are welcome.

Our goal

Help every member speak with more confidence in daily life, school, and work.

Our style

Mistakes are normal here. We correct kindly and celebrate progress.
