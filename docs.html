New Member Guide

Everything you need to feel at home at Del English Club—from your first meeting to finding the activity that fits your goals.

👋

New here?

Start with our member introduction session, meet the community, and tell us about your interests and current English level. You don't need to be fluent to join.

🧭

Choose your path

Build test skills with TOEFL Prep, focus alongside friends at Study Night, practice presenting, join debates, or develop your writing.

📅

Meeting schedule

Our proposed semester schedule starts Friday, 16 October 2026, and runs through 18 December. The committee will announce the meeting time and location.

Our philosophy: progress over perfection.

Try speaking English, ask questions, make mistakes, and learn from one another. Every member should feel respected and included.

How do I get started?
  • Check the Schedule section for the upcoming activity.
  • Follow the club's official communication channel for meeting time, location, and announcements.
  • Come ready to participate—bring any materials requested for that session.
  • Introduce yourself to the committee or a fellow member if you need help.
Club community guidelines
  • Be respectful of every member, accent, background, and English level.
  • Do not mock mistakes. Give feedback kindly and constructively.
  • Give everyone a chance to speak and listen without interrupting.
  • Try to use English during activities; helping someone find a word is encouraged.
  • Respect members' privacy. Ask permission before sharing photos, recordings, or their work publicly.
  • Keep discussions friendly and disagree with ideas—not with people.
What should I bring?
  • TOEFL Prep: notebook, pen, and any practice materials shared by the facilitator.
  • Study Night: your own assignment, notes, laptop or stationery as needed, and one realistic study goal.
  • Public Speaking: a topic or prompt if announced; beginners can start with a short introduction.
  • Debate: an open mind and willingness to support opinions with reasons and evidence.
  • Writing: notebook or laptop and a willingness to draft, revise, and give supportive feedback.
Can beginners join?

Absolutely. Activities are designed to help members grow at different levels. You can start with short contributions and take on bigger challenges as your confidence improves.

Is there a membership fee?

Membership fees and any other costs have not been specified in this website draft. Please confirm the current policy with the club committee.

Where do I find meeting updates?

The exact meeting time, room, and official contact details have not been confirmed in this draft. Check announcements from the club committee before attending.

Can my work be posted online?

Selected writing or activity highlights may be considered for the club's Instagram or website. The club should obtain the author's permission before publishing their work, name, photo, or recording.
