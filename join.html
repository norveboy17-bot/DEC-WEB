About the club

We're a student-run club that meets every week to speak English together in a relaxed, no-pressure setting. No tests, no grades. Just conversation, games, and learning from each other.

Who can join

Anyone who wants to improve. Beginners to advanced speakers are welcome.

Our goal

Help every member speak with more confidence in daily life, school, and work.

Our style

Mistakes are normal here. We correct kindly and celebrate progress.

What we do

Every session mixes a few of these, so no two weeks feel the same.

Conversation circles

Small groups chat about a new topic each week.

Debates and discussions

Practice sharing opinions and listening to others.

Games and challenges

Word games, storytelling, and quick quizzes.

Movie and music nights

Learn natural English from films and songs.

How to join
  • Fill in the form below.
  • Come to any session. Your first one is a free trial.
  • Meet the team and get added to the member group chat.
Club rules
  • Speak English during sessions, even if it's slow.
  • Be respectful. No mocking anyone's accent or mistakes.
  • Let everyone have a turn to talk.
  • Keep phones away unless the activity needs them.
Member guide

New members get a welcome pack with a vocabulary list, the term calendar, and tips for getting the most out of each session. Ask a team member for your copy.

Roles and committee
  • President: leads the club and plans each term.
  • Vice president: supports events and new members.
  • Secretary: handles announcements and records.
  • Activities lead: designs sessions and games.
